How to add jigsaw regions
  • Hold left click to add new region.
  • Right click region to remove it.
  • Every time you add the last region, the sudoku task will update.
  • Press the View button to view current sudoku task.
Region rules
  • All regions must have N cells.
  • All cells in a region must be orthogonally connected.
  • Each cell of sudoku must be part of exactly one region.
Ok
How to add extra regions
  • Hold left click to add new extra region.
  • Right click region to remove it.
Region rules
  • All regions must have N cells.
  • All cells in a region must be orthogonally connected.
  • Two regions can not intersect.
  • Unlike jigsaw regions, extra regions don't have to fill every cell.
Ok
What are sudoku regions
In a sudoku region, every number has to appear exactly once.
You can add regions of 3 main types: regular, diagonal and extra regions.
Every row and column of sudoku also counts as a region,
so each row and column must have every number exactly once as well.
Ok
What are sudoku numbers
You can fill some cells with a number.
Left click on a cell to choose it.
Press a number key to fill the cell with a number.
Press Backspace or Delete to remove a number from the cell.
For numbers 10 or higher use +10 button.
For numbers 10 or higher use +10 and +20 buttons.
Objective of solving sudoku is to fill all other numbers, but you can not change numbers in task.
