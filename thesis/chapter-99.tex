\chaptoc{Conclusion}

The main objective of this thesis was to develop software that helps people solve Sudoku puzzles
by applying strategies from simplest to most complex.
This objective was achieved, the software supports multiple types of Sudoku regions and five Sudoku variants.

Chapter~\ref{chap:5} explains and proves an algorithm that finds the smallest application of
Naked Candidates, Hidden Candidates, and Generalised X-Wing in polynomial time.
This was not part of the original objectives but became significant after the algorithm was implemented.

This thesis could be extended in the future by adding more variants and strategies.
